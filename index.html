How it works

Drop a film negative scan. Rust in your browser decodes it, WebGL inverts it. Nothing uploaded.

Pipeline

DecodeRust → WASM
InvertLog-space density
AdjustWebGL2 shader
ExportFull-res PNG

Density inversion: D = log(base) − log(pixel) per channel. Removes the C41 orange mask and inverts tonality in one op. All tone/crop/rotate is a single WebGL2 fragment pass, so sliders update at 60fps.

Formats

RAW: DNG, NEF (non-HE*), CR2/CR3, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2. Standard: JPEG, PNG, WebP. Nikon HE/HE* NEFs need Adobe's free DNG Converter first.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your device. Static site, no backend. Sessions persist in local IndexedDB.

Open source at github.com/henryfradley/film-negative-converter
